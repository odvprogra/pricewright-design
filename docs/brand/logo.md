# Logo

The ledger sheet: a ruled page with a folded corner, closed by the accountant's double rule under a
total ([ADR-0003](../adr/0003-the-ledger-brand-direction.md)). The wordmark is Source Serif 4
Subhead Semibold, outlined, so the logo shows the same everywhere without the font.

![The Pricewright logo](logo/pricewright-logo.svg)

fails if a committed file differs from what the script draws. Never edit them by hand.

## Construction

- **Grid:** the mark is 32 units square. The sheet is 19 by 24 units with a 6-unit fold; two rules
  of 11 units sit in its upper half; the double rule, 7 units wide, in the accent color, closes it.
- **Wordmark:** 25 units, 7 units right of the mark. Its baseline is the double rule's lower line,
  so the word sits on the total, as an amount does.
- **Small mark:** below 24 px the thin rules blur, so the sheet becomes solid with the fold and the
  double rule cut out.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space:** at least a quarter of the logo's height (8 units) on every side, free of text,
  edges and other marks.
- **Minimum size:** the logo and the mark at 24 px high; under that, only the small mark, down to 16
  px.

## Color

| On                            | Use                       | Contrast (tested)                           |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| White, `color.surface.muted`  | Navy and verdigris        | Ink 13.6:1, rule 5.5:1 on white; 3:1 needed |
| Navy, `color.surface.inverse` | White and light verdigris | Ink 13.6:1, rule 5.8:1; 3:1 needed          |

Anything else, photographs and tenant colors included, gets neither: place the logo on white or
navy.

## Misuse

- Do not recolor it, apart from the two variants above, and never in a tenant's accent.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, shadow or animate it.
- Do not set the wordmark in another font or retype it; use the files.
- Do not change the order or the spacing of the mark and the wordmark.
- Do not use the outlined mark below 24 px; use the small mark.
- Do not put it inside a shape or a badge other than an app icon's square.
