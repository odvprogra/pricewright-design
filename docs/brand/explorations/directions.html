Three directions for Pricewright

Pricewright prices B2B quotes through a transparent waterfall of discounts, sends them for approval and keeps the record. Each direction below reads that story differently. Pick one, or name the parts you want to combine; the chosen one becomes the design tokens, with every pair tested for contrast.

Direction A

Waterfall

Pricewright as a precision instrument. The logomark is a pocket-price waterfall: list price, two discounts, the net price. Cobalt on cool slate, IBM Plex with its monospace for SKUs and amounts. Feels engineered, exact and calm.

Strengths

  • The mark explains the product: the waterfall is the pricing engine.
  • Numbers-first type suits dense tables.
  • Neutral enough for any tenant accent.

Risks

  • Blue is the most common SaaS color; it leans on the mark to stand out.
  • Can feel cold to sales reps.

References: the pocket-price waterfall (Marn and Rosiello, “Managing Price, Gaining Profit”, HBR, 1992); IBM’s Carbon design system, built on Plex.

Direction B

Workshop

Pricewright as the craftsman in its name. The mark is a P cut square, its bowl a price tag with the hole punched through. Spruce green and brass on stone, a firm grotesque for headings and Source Sans for reading. Feels skilled, human and direct.

Strengths

  • Distinct in a category of blues (Salesforce, SAP, Oracle).

References: the name’s rationale (“wright” as craftsman, decision D-13); Shopify Polaris, a green brand with a practical voice.

Direction C

Ledger

Pricewright as the document of record. The mark is a ruled sheet closed by the accountant’s double rule under the total. Navy and verdigris on white paper, Source Serif for headings and Public Sans for the interface. Feels institutional, careful and permanent.

  1. The chosen direction becomes the design tokens: primitives, then semantic names, each pair tested for contrast in CI.
  2. The logo is refined at its real sizes (favicon, app header, PDF), with clear space, minimum size and misuse.
  3. Northfield Supply and Larkspur Tool Co. get their logos and accents, checked against the brand.
  4. The brand guide, the screen specs and the clickable prototype are all built on those tokens.
