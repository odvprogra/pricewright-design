# Usability test 1 — questionnaires

## After each task: Single Ease Question (SEQ)

Asked right after each task, aloud, the answer written in the task sheet
([MeasuringU](https://measuringu.com/seq10/)).

- English: "Overall, how difficult or easy was this task to complete?" 1 = very difficult, 7 = very
  easy.
- Spanish (as spoken in the sessions): "En general, ¿qué tan difícil o fácil fue completar esta
  tarea?" 1 = muy difícil, 7 = muy fácil.

The SEQ has no validated Spanish version that we know of; the translation above is the moderator's,
and the report says so.

## At the end: System Usability Scale (SUS)

The participant answers on their own, in a form or on paper, without discussing the items. The
Spanish version is the one validated by Sevilla-Gonzalez et al. (2020), reproduced verbatim from its
Multimedia Appendix 2 under CC BY 4.0: Sevilla-Gonzalez MDR, Moreno Loaeza L, Lazaro-Carrera LS, et
al., "Spanish Version of the System Usability Scale for the Assessment of Electronic Tools:
Development and Validation", _JMIR Human Factors_ 7(4):e21161, 2020,
<https://doi.org/10.2196/21161>. Its wording is kept as validated, including item 9's "confiado";
the original English items are John Brooke's (1996).

Instructions given to the participant (Spanish, verbatim):

> Por favor seleccione de cada uno de los enunciados la opción que mejor describa su experiencia con
> la herramienta electrónica. Un puntaje de 1 significa que usted se encuentra totalmente en
> desacuerdo con el enunciado, mientras que un puntaje en 5 significa que está totalmente de
> acuerdo, un puntaje de 3 significaría que usted se encuentra neutral con el enunciado.

Scale: 1 (Totalmente en desacuerdo / Strongly disagree) to 5 (Totalmente de acuerdo / Strongly
agree).

| #   | Spanish (validated)                                                                     | English (Brooke)                                                                           |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Me gustaría usar esta herramienta frecuentemente.                                       | I think that I would like to use this system frequently.                                   |
| 2   | Considero que esta herramienta es innecesariamente compleja.                            | I found the system unnecessarily complex.                                                  |
| 3   | Considero que la herramienta es fácil de usar.                                          | I thought the system was easy to use.                                                      |
| 4   | Considero necesario el apoyo de personal experto para poder utilizar esta herramienta.  | I think that I would need the support of a technical person to be able to use this system. |
| 5   | Considero que las funciones de la herramienta están bien integradas.                    | I found the various functions in this system were well integrated.                         |
| 6   | Considero que la herramienta presenta muchas contradicciones.                           | I thought there was too much inconsistency in this system.                                 |
| 7   | Imagino que la mayoría de las personas aprenderían a usar esta herramienta rápidamente. | I would imagine that most people would learn to use this system very quickly.              |
| 8   | Considero que el uso de esta herramienta es tedioso.                                    | I found the system very cumbersome to use.                                                 |
| 9   | Me sentí muy confiado al usar la herramienta.                                           | I felt very confident using the system.                                                    |
| 10  | Necesité saber bastantes cosas antes de poder empezar a usar esta herramienta.          | I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with this system.               |

### Scoring

For each participant: odd items score `answer − 1`, even items `5 − answer`; the sum times 2.5 is
the SUS score, from 0 to 100. Every item must be answered; a missing answer is asked for before the
participant leaves. The report gives each participant's score, the mean and its 95% confidence
interval (t distribution, 4 degrees of freedom), and compares the mean with 68, the average across
products ([MeasuringU](https://measuringu.com/sus/)).
